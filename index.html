SoFilNet: Self-Supervised Source-Filter Model of Speech Production

Audio samples

SoFilNet estimates, for every 10 ms frame, 28 interpretable parameters of a source-filter model of speech: F0, voicing, five band aperiodicities and a gain for the glottal source, and the radii and frequencies of eight pole pairs and two zero pairs for the vocal-tract filter. It is trained on TIMIT without any F0, formant or voicing labels. The samples below show how much of the speech signal these parameters retain (Table V of the paper).

Conditions

Original
The input recording.
Parameters only
Synthesized from the 28 estimated parameters per frame alone: harmonic + noise source through the pole-zero filter.
Estimated magnitude, original phase
Estimated spectrum combined with the phase of the recording.
Original magnitude, estimated phase
Spectrum of the recording combined with the phase of the parameter-only synthesis.
